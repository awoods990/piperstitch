Formats & machines

The file your machine reads.

PiperStitch writes native machine formats directly — no converter in between — and reads them back for review.

Export

DSTTajima and the de-facto commercial standard — read by nearly every commercial machine (Tajima, Barudan, SWF, ZSK, Ricoma, Happy…) and most home machines. Carries stitches, jumps, trims and colour-change stops; colours themselves are set on the machine.
PESBrother and Baby Lock home and semi-commercial machines (PE, SE, NQ, Innov-ís, Persona, Entrepreneur…). Includes the PEC stitch block plus the Brother thread palette, so the machine shows the right colours.
JEFJanome (and Elna) machines. Written with the Janome thread palette and the hoop information Janome machines expect.
EXPMelco and Bernina-compatible machines. Stitches, jumps and colour changes; colours are set on the machine.
.stitchpilotPiperStitch's own project file: your artwork, every object with its settings, and the sew order. Reopen it to keep editing.

Import

PNG, JPEGAny raster image. Colours are quantised to the number the design really uses; each colour becomes traced objects with their holes intact.
SVGVector artwork comes in as exact paths — the best starting point if you have it.
DST, PES, JEF, EXPExisting stitch files can be opened for preview and readiness review.

Hoops

Built-in sizes4″×4″, 5″×7″, 6″×10″, 8″×8″, 9″×9″, 10″×10″. The canvas shows the hoop boundary and warns when a design won't fit.
Garment sizesStandard left-chest, cap-front and full-front sizes are a click away when setting the finished size.
Not yet: VP3 (Husqvarna Viking / Pfaff), XXX (Singer), HUS and ART. If your machine reads only one of these, most free converters handle DST → VP3/XXX well, and these formats are on the roadmap. Tell us which you need: contact@piperstitch.com.

Thread libraries

Real thread numbers, not RGB guesses.

PiperStitch ships the full color catalog for eight major thread makers — Isacord, Madeira (Polyneon & Rayon), Robison-Anton, Sulky, Hemingworth, Floriani, Marathon and Gunold. Pick your manufacturer and every shade they sell is right there, ready to search; add the handful you actually keep on the rack, or grab the whole line in one click and prune later. Nothing is ever locked in — add, remove or rebuild your library any time.

  • 4,600+ real manufacturer colors, searchable by name or number
  • One-click Add, or Add all to bring in an entire manufacturer's line at once
  • Exported files carry your library's thread numbers — the exact spool to load, not just a color swatch
  • Thread weight (standard 40wt or fine 60wt) adjusts fill density automatically, from your library or a one-off choice

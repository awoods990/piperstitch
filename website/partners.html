The fine print, in plain words

What we’ll hold you to, and what we hold ourselves to.

  • The clock starts at first payment, not at signup, and runs 24 months. A referral who cancels and comes back resumes inside the same window; it never restarts.
  • A typed code beats a link. Someone who clicked your link gets 90 days for it to count; someone who types a code is attributed to that code, whoever’s link they clicked. One partner per customer, settled at first payment.
  • Refunds and chargebacks reverse the matching commission. A cancellation or refund within 60 days of first payment also reverses that referral’s $15 bounty. Commission becomes payable 60 days after the invoice for the same reason.
  • Your rate is yours. It’s written on your account at approval and only changes if we agree it with you. Changing the program later never changes existing partners.
  • Disclosure is required, not optional. The FTC holds us responsible for what our partners say. “Affiliate link” on its own isn’t enough; the kit lists what is. No income claims, no “perfect every time”, no comparisons by name to other software.
  • You never see who signed up. The portal shows dates, statuses and amounts — not names or emails. That’s a promise we make to your audience in our privacy policy.
  • You’re an independent contractor. We need a W-9 (US) or W-8BEN before the first payout, and we issue a 1099-NEC to US partners paid $600 or more in a year.

Questions

The ones partners ask first.

Do I need to be a PiperStitch subscriber?

You need to have used it — partners only talk about what they’ve tried, and your audience will know the difference. The free trial is enough to start; most partners end up subscribing because they’re using it on real orders.

Can I get more than one code?

Yes. Many partners keep one code per channel — KATHLEEN-YT and KATHLEEN-FB, say — so the portal shows which one converts. Reply to any of our emails and we’ll add it.

Does the 30% apply to Proofs too?

Yes — to every invoice the referred customer pays, on both the PiperStitch subscription and the Proofs add-on, computed from the actual amount. It’s a share of what they pay, before payment-processing fees, with no footnote.

When and how am I paid?

By PayPal, on the last day of each month, for everything that became payable in the month before. Commission becomes payable 60 days after the invoice it comes from (the refund window). Balances under $50 roll over to the next month. We need your W-9 or W-8BEN on file first.

What if someone I referred cancels?

You keep everything already earned, except where the invoice itself is refunded or charged back. If they cancel or refund within 60 days of their first payment, the $15 bounty for that person is reversed. If they come back later, commission resumes inside the original 24-month window.

What can’t I say?

Anything you haven’t seen yourself; “perfect every time” or “never needs editing”; that it replaces a professional digitizer; comparisons by name to other digitizing software; any income claim for your viewers; and calling it AI (it’s rules-based, which is why the same artwork gives the same file every time). The portal has the full list, with what you can say.

Why isn’t the rate on this page?

Because it’s a commercial arrangement, not an advertisement. Our subscribers shouldn’t have to wonder what the person recommending us is earning before they’ve even been told there’s a program. Register above and you’ll have every figure a second later — we’re not being coy, just tidy.

Is registering the same as applying?

No. Registering just opens the details. The application is at the bottom of that page, once you know what you’d be signing up to, and we answer every one ourselves within a few days.

You need to have used PiperStitch — the free trial is enough to start. Most partners end up subscribing because they’re using it on real orders, which is rather the point.

How the billing works

No surprises.

No card to try it

The free trial runs for 14 days from your first sign-in. You're never asked for payment details until you decide to subscribe.

Stripe handles your card

Payment happens on Stripe's secure checkout page. PiperStitch never sees or stores your card number. Renewals are charged automatically each month on the day you subscribed.

Proofs is added from inside the app

Use your three included proofs first. When you want more, Settings in Proofs adds the $24 subscription to the same card and the same monthly bill — one account, one invoice.

Manage it yourself

Update your card, download invoices, or cancel — from your account page, any time, no email required. A failed renewal gives you a few days' grace to fix the card before anything locks.

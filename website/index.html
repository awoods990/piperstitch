The difference

Not a tracer. A digitizer.

Most “auto-convert” tools turn pixels into stitches and leave you with a design that puckers, gaps, and looks nothing like the picture. PiperStitch works the way a professional digitizer does: it breaks your artwork into objects, picks the right stitch technique for each one, adds underlay and compensation, orders the sew-out, simulates the result — and then audits it for real embroidery problems before you ever thread a needle.

How it works

Four steps. The hard ones are automatic.

1

Open your artwork

Drag in a logo, a drawing, a photo of a sketch — PNG, JPEG, or SVG. PiperStitch separates it into colors and shapes and recommends a finished size based on the finest detail it finds.

2

Say where it's going

Pick the hoop, the fabric (twill, pique, fleece, stretch knit, terry…) and the finished size in centimeters. Compensation and underlay adjust to the fabric automatically.

3

Click Create Embroidery File

Each object gets the stitch type a digitizer would choose, with underlay, pull compensation, curvature-aware satin density and hidden travel routing. Lettering is generated from real fonts, not traced.

4

Check the preview, export, sew

A realistic stitch simulation and an Embroidery Readiness report show you exactly what the machine will do. Export DST, PES, JEF or EXP and load it on your machine.

The difference

Not a tracer. A digitizer.

Most “auto-convert” tools turn pixels into stitches and leave you with a design that puckers, gaps, and looks nothing like the picture. PiperStitch works the way an experienced human digitizer does — the person a shop would pay to hand-build the design: it breaks your artwork into objects, picks the right stitch technique for each one, adds underlay and compensation, orders the sew-out, simulates the result — and then audits it for real embroidery problems before you ever thread a needle.

And it isn’t AI guessing, either. PiperStitch is a rules-based digitizer: every decision follows the same embroidery rules a professional learns — minimum satin width, density for the fabric, underlay for the stitch type, pull compensation for the direction of travel. The same artwork gives the same file every time, every choice is shown to you with the reason, and nothing you upload is used to train anything.

How it works

Six steps. The hard ones are automatic.

1

Open your artwork

Drag in a logo, a drawing, a photo of a sketch — PNG, JPEG, or SVG. PiperStitch separates it into colors and shapes and recommends a finished size based on the finest detail it finds.

2

Say where it's going

Pick the hoop, the fabric (twill, pique, fleece, stretch knit, terry…) and the finished size in centimeters. Compensation and underlay adjust to the fabric automatically.

3

Click Create Embroidery File

Each object gets the stitch type a digitizer would choose, with underlay, pull compensation, curvature-aware satin density and hidden travel routing. Lettering is generated from real fonts, not traced.

4

Check the preview

A realistic stitch simulation and an Embroidery Readiness report show you exactly what the machine will do — and flag anything that won’t sew well before you find out on fabric.

5

Send the proof Proofs

Sewing it for someone? Click Send to Proofs. The proof builds itself from the real stitch file — the render, the thread stops, the size on the garment — and goes out as one link they open on their phone.

6

Export and sew when they say yes

Approval unlocks the run ticket and writes the certificate. Export DST, PES, JEF, EXP or VP3 and load it on your machine — or skip the proof and export straight away when it’s for you.

Does the work · leaves you in control

It decides. You get the final say.

PiperStitch chooses the stitch type, density, underlay, compensation and sew order for every shape — and then shows you every one of those decisions, ready to change. Real digitizing tools, not a black box: switch a shape from fill to satin, turn the fill angle, tighten the density, reorder the sequence, merge or paint shapes, place lettering in any font, pick threads from your machine’s own palette. The realistic preview and the readiness score follow every move, so you can dial the file in to exactly what you intend before a single stitch is sewn.

  • Per-object stitch type, density, underlay, angle and compensation
  • Merge, paint, erase, move and resize directly on the canvas
  • Sew-order control, thread palettes, project save and undo

1. PIPERSTITCH AND HOW IT WORKS

1.1 A Browser-Based Service

PiperStitch is an automatic embroidery digitizing application delivered through a web browser. Nothing is downloaded or installed on the User’s computer. Unlike software that runs locally, PiperStitch’s digitizing engine operates on servers operated by or for Piperstitch LLC. Artwork that a User imports into PiperStitch is transmitted to those servers, converted into stitch data, and returned to the User’s browser, where the resulting design is displayed, edited, and downloaded.

Piperstitch LLC does not retain copies of downloaded files, except as part of a proof record as described in Section 10.

Proofs is an optional add-on to PiperStitch through which User can collect artwork and job details from the people User embroiders for (“Recipients”), send them a proof rendered from a Design, capture their approval or requested changes, and generate a Certificate of Approval and production documents.

1. The short version

  • Your artwork and your embroidery designs never leave your Mac. PiperStitch does no cloud processing.
  • We collect your name and email when you register to download, and your name, email and payment record (via Stripe) when you subscribe.
  • The App sends us your email address and an anonymous identifier for your Mac when you sign in and when it checks your subscription in the background. Nothing else.
  • We do not sell or share your information for advertising. There are no ad trackers on the Site or in the App.

2. What we collect and why

When you register to download: first and last name, email address, optionally your machine model and business name, the version of the Terms you accepted, the time, and your IP address. We use this to send your download link and trial reminder, to know who accepted which Terms, and to connect a later subscription to your record.

When you subscribe: name and email, and from Stripe a record of each payment (amount, date, last four card digits and card brand, invoice id). Stripe collects your full card details directly on its own pages; we never receive them.

When you use the App while signed in: your email address, a randomly generated identifier for your Mac, and a name for it (taken from macOS, e.g. “Jane’s MacBook Pro”), so you can see and sign out your Macs. The App periodically asks our server whether your subscription is active; that request contains only the device token. It never contains your artwork, designs, files, or anything about what you are working on.

When you email us: whatever you send, so we can reply.

3. What we do not collect

  • Your artwork, designs, projects or exported files. Digitizing, preview and export are performed entirely on your Mac.
  • Usage analytics, crash reports or telemetry from the App.
  • Advertising identifiers or third-party tracking cookies on the Site.

4. Cookies

The Site uses one session cookie to unlock the download after registration, and the account page uses one session cookie to keep you signed in for the length of your visit. Neither is used for tracking. Stripe’s checkout and billing pages set their own cookies under Stripe’s policy.

5. Who we share it with

Service providers who process data on our behalf, only as needed to run the Service: Stripe (payments), our email provider (sending the emails described above), and our web and server hosts. We may also disclose information if required by law or to protect the rights and safety of PiperStitch or others. We do not sell personal information.

6. Email

We send transactional email: download links, sign-in codes, account-page links, welcome and billing notices, and a reminder before your trial ends. We may occasionally send product news to people who registered or subscribed; every such email has an unsubscribe link, and sign-in and billing emails are unaffected by unsubscribing.

7. How long we keep it

Registration and subscription records are kept while you have an account with us and for as long afterwards as we need them for tax, accounting and legal purposes. Sign-in codes and account links expire within minutes and are then useless. You can ask us to delete your record at any time (see Your rights); we will do so unless we are required to keep part of it.

8. Security

Data is transmitted over HTTPS. Sign-in codes, account links and device tokens are stored only as one-way hashes. Registration records on the Site are kept outside the web root. No method is perfect; if we learn of a breach affecting you, we will tell you.

9. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete the personal information we hold about you, or to object to or restrict certain processing. Email hello@piperstitch.com and we will respond within 30 days. You may also complain to your local data-protection authority.

10. Children

The Service is for adults. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 18; if you believe we have, tell us and we will delete it.

11. International users

PiperStitch operates from the United States and our providers are located there. By using the Service you understand that your information is processed in the United States.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy; the current version and its date are always at piperstitch.com/privacy-policy.html. For material changes we will email registered users.

13. Contact

hello@piperstitch.com.

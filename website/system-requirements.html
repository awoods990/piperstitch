System requirements

What PiperStitch needs.

A Mac with Apple silicon. That's most of it.

Mac

ProcessorApple silicon — M1, M2, M3, M4 or later, any variant. Intel Macs are not supported.
Operating systemmacOS 13 Ventura or later. macOS 14 Sonoma or 15 Sequoia recommended.
Memory8 GB is enough. Large, detailed artwork at big finished sizes is more comfortable with 16 GB.
DiskUnder 100 MB for the app. Projects and exports are small.
DisplayAny. Retina displays show the realistic thread preview at its best.

Internet

To download and sign inYes — briefly. The sign-in code arrives by email, and the app checks your subscription in the background when it can.
To digitizeNo. Import, digitizing, preview and export all run on your Mac. A signed-in copy keeps working offline for weeks between checks.

Version

Current releasePiperStitch 0.1.0. The app notices new versions on its own and offers a download link; updates are free while you're subscribed.
InstallationOpen the disk image and drag PiperStitch to Applications. The first time you open it, macOS may ask you to confirm — right-click the app and choose Open.

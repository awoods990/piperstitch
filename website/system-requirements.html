What you need

A web browser. That's it.

PiperStitch runs entirely in your browser — nothing to download, nothing to install, nothing to update.

Computer

Works onMac, Windows PC, Chromebook, iPad or phone — anything that runs a current browser. On a laptop you get the full toolbars; on an iPad or phone the layout switches to touch controls, pinch-to-zoom and a slide-up inspector, and you can photograph artwork with the camera and digitize it on the spot. Fine detail work is easier with a mouse or trackpad.
BrowserChrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox, a version from the last two years. Chrome or Edge give the smoothest canvas.
ScreenAny. A laptop-size screen or larger is comfortable for the editor; the realistic thread preview looks best on a high-resolution display.
MemoryWhatever your browser is already happy with. The heavy work — digitizing — happens on our servers, not your computer.

Internet

To sign inYes. The six-digit sign-in code arrives by email.
To digitizeYes. Your artwork is sent to PiperStitch’s server, digitized in under a second, and the stitches come straight back. Ordinary home broadband or a phone hotspot is plenty.
Your filesEvery DST, PES, JEF, EXP or VP3 file downloads to your computer and is yours to keep — copy it to a USB stick or send it to your machine the way you always have.

Your embroidery machine

FormatsTajima DST, Brother/Baby Lock PES, Janome JEF, Melco/Bernina EXP, Husqvarna Viking/Pfaff VP3. See Formats & Machines for which one your machine reads.
HoopsStandard sizes from 4″ × 4″ up to 10″ × 10″, cap frames and magnetic hoops — PiperStitch checks your design fits before you download.

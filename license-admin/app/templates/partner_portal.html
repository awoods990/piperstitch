    {% for d in disclosure_inadequate %}
  • “{{ d }}”{% if d == 'affiliate link' %} on its own{% endif %}

It also has to be where they’ll actually see it: spoken and on screen inside a video rather than only in the description, left up long enough to read in a Story, and repeated every so often during a livestream. When in doubt, say more rather than less — nobody has ever lost an audience by being straight with them.
